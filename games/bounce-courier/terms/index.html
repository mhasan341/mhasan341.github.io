Purchases & Courier Gems

The game offers optional gem packs, cosmetics, and ad-related upgrades. The App Store purchase screen shows the current product, price, currency, and applicable conditions before you confirm. Prices can vary by region. A temporary ad-free benefit and a permanent ad-free purchase have different durations, as described in the offer.

Courier Gems are virtual items for use inside BounceCourier. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn as money, and are not intended for sale or transfer outside the game. Purchased gems do not expire. Spending gems uses the corresponding amount from your balance.

Eligible non-consumable purchases can be restored using the Apple Account that bought them. Gem packs are consumable purchases: restoring purchases refreshes available entitlements and the accessible saved gem wallet; it does not replenish gems already spent. In version 1.1, a secure device wallet can be used without iCloud. After a wallet is transferred to iCloud, access to that cloud wallet requires the original iCloud account and a working connection. Wallet sync is separate from the progress-sync setting.

If a purchase is charged but missing, use Restore purchases and contact support before buying it again. Refunds for purchases billed by Apple can be requested through Apple’s refund service. Eligibility is determined by Apple’s process and applicable law. An approved refund or reversed transaction may result in the related entitlement or gem credit being adjusted.

Ads & optional rewards

BounceCourier may show ads between completed runs. Optional rewarded ads let you choose a displayed in-game benefit. A permanent ad-free purchase removes mandatory ads; optional reward videos remain available when offered.

A reward requires completing the ad’s stated requirements and confirmation from the advertising service. Closing an ad early may not earn the reward. Ad availability depends on factors such as your region, connection, consent settings, and provider inventory. Daily limits and reward amounts are shown in the game and can change for future offers.

Third-party ads and linked destinations are provided by their respective advertisers. You can report an inappropriate or broken ad to us. Privacy and consent information is covered in our Privacy Policy.

Progress, iCloud & Game Center

Beginner, Expert, and Pro have separate progression. Game Center features depend on Apple’s service and your account settings. Scores and achievements are submitted when the game’s eligibility rules are met and a connection is available.

Where enabled, progress sync uses iCloud. Version 1.1 can keep gems in a secure device wallet without iCloud. When transferred to iCloud, that wallet uses the original iCloud account, which may be different from the Apple Account used for App Store purchases. Cloud features need a supported account and connection; synchronization may take time or require a retry.

Resetting progress and deleting game data are different actions. Read the in-game confirmation carefully: deletion can remove saved progress and gem-wallet data, while Apple purchase records and previously submitted Game Center records may remain. Please contact support if you need help before resetting, deleting, or changing accounts.
