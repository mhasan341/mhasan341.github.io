The role

Make the next player curious.

We’re looking for a Digital Marketing Expert to plan, create and improve the content that promotes our games. You’ll help shape our social presence, turn real gameplay into compelling stories, and connect with players across Instagram, TikTok and other relevant channels.

What you’ll do

  • Plan and manage a content calendar for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and other channels that fit our games.
  • Capture gameplay and create short videos, Reels, captions, thumbnails and simple graphics, from the opening hook to the call to action.
  • Build launch campaigns and ongoing content around updates, gameplay moments, challenges and community stories.
  • Engage with players, respond thoughtfully to comments and help turn useful feedback into ideas.
  • Find relevant creators and gaming communities, and coordinate clear, professional outreach and collaborations.
  • Track reach, watch time, engagement, store visits and installs where measurement is available; report what worked and test better ideas.

What you’ll bring

  • Hands-on experience growing or managing social accounts, with examples of your content and results.
  • A strong understanding of Instagram Reels, TikTok and short-form video: hooks, pacing, captions, trends and audience behaviour.
  • Video editing skills in tools such as CapCut, Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, plus basic graphic design in Canva, Figma or similar tools.
  • Clear English writing, a feel for a playful brand voice and the ability to explain a game in a few words.
  • Comfort with social analytics, content experiments, link tracking and practical performance reports.
  • Reliable communication, organised remote work and the ability to take an idea from brief to finished post.
  • Good judgement about platform rules, copyright, licensed music and clear disclosures for paid collaborations.

Nice extras

Mobile-game marketing, app-store optimisation, Discord community building, creator partnerships, or running and measuring Meta/TikTok ads with an agreed budget.

Your application

Show us what you make.

Apply through LinkedIn with your CV or professional profile, links to relevant content or accounts you’ve worked on, and a short note about your approach to marketing games.

Include results you’re allowed to share, your availability and expected salary. Salary is negotiable based on experience and the agreed scope.
